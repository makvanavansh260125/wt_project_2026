Our Legacy & Heritage

Pioneering Memorable Holidays Since 2001

Founded with an enduring passion to make travel enriching, comfortable, and accessible to everyone, Akshar Tours & Travels has blossomed from a boutique travel agency in Gujarat into one of Western India's most respected full-service travel management houses.

Whether you dream of waking up to prayer bells along the Ghats of Varanasi, cruising through mist-draped Norwegian fjords, or gliding on a private shikara across Dal Lake, our veteran destination planners craft personalized experiences that surpass expectations.

Guiding Principles

Our Vision, Mission & Values

Our Vision

To be the most trusted, innovative, and customer-centric travel management company in India, empowering travelers of every generation to explore the globe with joy, confidence, and peace of mind.
