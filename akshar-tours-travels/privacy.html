Last Updated: November 2026 | Compliant with Global Privacy Regulations

1. Information We Collect

At Akshar Tours & Travels, we collect personal details necessary to fulfill travel bookings and provide customer support, including your full name, email address, mobile number, postal address, passport details (for international visa and flight ticketing), and special travel or dietary requests.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is exclusively utilized for:

  • Issuing travel vouchers, flight e-tickets, and hotel confirmation receipts.
  • Communicating schedule modifications, itinerary updates, and gate details.
  • Filing visa applications and consular paperwork on your behalf.
  • Sending customized promotional deals and newsletters (opt-out anytime).

3. Data Security & LocalStorage Policy

This web portal demonstrates modern travel architecture. Session and booking records are stored in browser LocalStorage for customer preview and demonstration management. In commercial production, end-to-end 256-bit SSL encryption and strict PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways protect all transactions.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We never sell or lease your personal information to marketing brokers. Necessary details are shared strictly with verified travel partners (airlines, hotels, transport providers, and visa processing consulates) exclusively to complete your travel reservations.

5. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

If you have any questions about this privacy statement or wish to request data deletion, contact us at privacy@akshartours.com or visit our head office on C.G. Road, Ahmedabad.
