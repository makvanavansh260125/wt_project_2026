Frequently Asked Questions

Browse our Indian or International packages, click "Book Now", choose your preferred transportation (Flight, Train, Bus for India; Flight or Cruise for International), select travel date, specify number of travelers, enter your details, and submit. You will instantly receive a unique Booking ID and a downloadable booking request voucher.

For all Indian packages, we offer three dynamic transportation modes: Flight, Train, and Luxury Bus. When you select a transportation mode, the estimated package price updates dynamically to reflect the respective fare adjustments.

In accordance with international cross-border tourism regulations and travel logistics, overseas packages feature strictly Flight (included in base price) and Luxury Cruise upgrade options. Train and bus modes are not offered for international destinations.

Cancellations made 30+ days prior to departure receive a 90% refund. Cancellations between 15-29 days receive a 75% refund. You can request a cancellation at any time directly through your Customer Dashboard or by contacting our 24/7 support line.
